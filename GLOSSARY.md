# Stephanie's Birthday Experience

A personalized birthday celebration centered on a cake and a candle-blowing interaction.

## Language

**Cake landing**:
The arrival of Stephanie's birthday cake and plate in their display position, marking the start of the birthday melody.
_Avoid_: Gift opening, birthday greeting reveal

**Birthday invitation**:
The opening welcome for Stephanie, featuring a gift and an invitation to enter her birthday celebration.
_Avoid_: Automatic countdown, birthday greeting reveal

**Celebrant**:
The person whose birthday the experience celebrates: Stephanie.
_Avoid_: Account, customer

**Successful blow**:
A recognized blowing interaction that extinguishes all lit candles together.
_Avoid_: Individual candle hit

**Manual blow**:
An intentional button action that extinguishes the candles without requiring a microphone.
_Avoid_: Fake microphone detection

**Birthday candles**:
The two numeral-shaped candles, "2" and "0", representing Stephanie's 20th birthday.
_Avoid_: Twenty individual candles, five regular candles

**Completed celebration**:
The ending with the greeting visible and both candles extinguished, after smoke and the brief confetti burst.
_Avoid_: Replay screen

**Birthday cats**:
The three feline companions in Stephanie's celebration: a black cat, a cream-white Siamese, and a white calico with orange and black patches.
_Avoid_: Three black cats

**Celebration music loop**:
The post-blow continuation of the birthday sound, allowed to repeat while the smoke, cat greeting, confetti, and final birthday message complete.
_Avoid_: Automatic music before the invitation, infinite audio after the celebration ends

**Envelope reveal**:
The centered final birthday presentation: a closed Sage Green + Cream + White pixel envelope with a small seal opens after confetti to reveal the persistent two-line message card.
_Avoid_: Bottom footer card, heavy blocking modal, replay screen
